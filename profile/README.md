# BlackArrows

**Super intelligence economy scarcity research.** Founded and run by **Hizrian Raz**.

> Scarcity is given. Price is an argument.

The super intelligence economy runs on a handful of things that cannot be wished into existence.
BlackArrows studies four of them, one at a time, as four separate markets:

| Book | Unit of scarcity |
|---|---|
| **Compute** | An hour of GPU time |
| **Memory** | DRAM / HBM capacity and bandwidth |
| **Metals** | Copper first, then the other conductors in the build-out |
| **Energy & Power** | Fuel at the wellhead, power at the node |

Each book is kept on its own page, on its own clock. A limit written in a rack, a die, a
mine or a grid is a fact. The price of that limit is an argument — and the argument is
the research.

## What BlackArrows is not

- Not a fund, and not a manager. It takes **no outside capital** and solicits none.
- Not trading. All four books are **paper only**: no live execution, no broker or
  exchange account, no orders.
- Not a benchmark or data vendor.
- No track record, and no figures published.
